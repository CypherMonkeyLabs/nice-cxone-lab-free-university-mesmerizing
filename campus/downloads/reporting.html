Widgets and the license gate

The Frustration widget is an Interaction Analytics widget. It needs the Interaction Analytics license plus Analytics > Dashboard Widgets, Dashboard view, and optionally Dashboard edit.

Organizations with a Performance Management (Native) license can see up to 95 days inside a 25-month lookback. A dashboard-only license is limited to same-day data, on that widget page.

If the organization is not configured for Dashboard, or the apps do not include ACD or QM, the dashboard permission page says those permissions will not appear.

Export

Analytics data export writes JSON to a secure external access bucket. Files go out every 400 records or every 6 minutes, whichever comes first.

The page warns that duplicates can exist and that order is processing order, not contact order. Do not build a deduplicated warehouse assumption on top of silence.
