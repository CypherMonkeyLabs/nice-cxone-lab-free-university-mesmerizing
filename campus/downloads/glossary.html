Glossary (first sheet)

The campus glossary is longer. This sheet is the first 28 terms. Each one links to its source from the full floor.

A leg. The portion of a call between the contact and NiCE CXone. On an inbound call it is ingress.

B leg. The portion of a call between NiCE CXone and the agent. A transfer adds a second B leg.

PSTN. On the connectivity page, phone traffic routed by DID or toll-free number.

DID. Direct Inward Dial. The connectivity page uses it as one of the two PSTN number types.

TFN. Toll-free number. The preferred path is to make CXone the RespOrg.

RespOrg. Responsible Organization for a toll-free number. If CXone is the RespOrg, you order more numbers through CXone.

Voice PoP. The carrier's physical gateway to the PSTN.

SIP trunk. A virtual PBX-to-carrier connection using a traditional line, VoIP, or a dedicated path such as MPLS.

Integrated Softphone. The built-in agent softphone. Nothing to install. It uses WebRTC. It is not the same name as WebRTC.

WebRTC. The communications technology in the Chrome browser that Integrated Softphone uses.

Point of contact. The entry, such as a phone number or email address. It is assigned one skill and one script.

ACD skill. The thing that automates delivery to an agent who can help. A campaign is required when you create one.

Overall priority. The static-delivery page's formula: (Time × Acceleration) + Initial Priority. Time is in minutes.

Proficiency. 1 is best and 20 is worst. Bullseye uses a high-to-low window.

Bullseye routing. Delivery that tightens or widens proficiency, or uses routing attributes, with actions such as REQAGENT and UpdateContact.

REQAGENT. The Studio action that requests an agent. A blank skill uses the point of contact's skill.

MENU. Stores the contact's choice in a variable. Prompts can be recorded, TTS, or both.

DNIS. The number dialed. Phone DNIS is configured by Professional Services before the point of contact.

Digital skill. The inbound digital path since the Summer 2023 change. New routing queues cannot be created.

Personal Connection. Proactive voice. Acceleration is set to 0 so the contact does not sit in an inbound-style queue. Enablement is at the business unit and the skill.

CXone WFM. Forecast, schedule, bids, time off, and adherence. The welcome page states a scale up to 10,000 agents.

IEX. A different workforce product. Its help is inside the IEX application.

AHT. On the forecast page, the average time to complete a transaction from start to finish.

ScreenAgent. Records and monitors the agent's screen. From version 2.1.40, a voice mask stops active screen recordings.

Hold gap. Hold audio, including hold music, is not recorded. A consultation during hold is recorded.

Engagement Hub. Brings third-party PABX, ACD, CRM, digital, and dialer data into CXone for Supervisor, Copilot for Supervisors, QM, and WFM.

Multi-ACD. Real-time recording of a third-party phone system. Versions live in that table, not in the WFM connector table.

Autopilot (Omilia). The virtual agent for voice and digital. Voice setup needs NiCE. Text needs Digital Experience.
