Amazon Connect

One page imports new voice and screen recordings into CXone for quality, analytics, and reporting. Users need an email address. Historical calls from before deployment are not the import the page describes.

A WFM guide describes an Engagement Hub connector that reads Kinesis and S3 and does not write back into the tenant.

A Multi-ACD page says you prepare AWS and a NiCE account representative finishes the CXone side.
