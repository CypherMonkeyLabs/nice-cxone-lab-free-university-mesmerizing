Three ways the pages talk about voice entry

PSTN, on the connectivity page, is phone traffic routed by DID or toll-free number.

A SIP trunk is a virtual connection between a PBX and a carrier, over a phone line, VoIP, or a dedicated path such as MPLS. SIP itself is RFC 3261. This lab does not invent CXone SIP headers.

The agent leg is separate: Integrated Softphone or the installed Softphone.

Toll-free ownership

If CXone is the RespOrg, you order more toll-free numbers through CXone. The IP address page says you must allow the blocks associated with those numbers and points you at the wizard.

Voice PoP, in the glossary this campus uses, is the carrier’s physical gateway. The exact PoP for a tenant is a wizard output, not a memorized city.
