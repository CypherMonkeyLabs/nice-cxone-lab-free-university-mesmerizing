Pick the move

Workforce data from Cisco UCCE or Avaya CMS is a WFM connector. The UCCE connector page and the Multi-ACD version table are different products and name different versions.

Live third-party phones recorded into CXone are Multi-ACD. Historical files are Migrated Calls and need the Migrated Interactions Upload license.

Replacing the ACD itself is not a procedure in these pages. Mark that line “ask the account team” instead of inventing weekends and freeze windows.

What you can schedule from the cited pages

Confirm the connector version on the connector’s own page. Do not copy the Multi-ACD table onto WFM.

For Migrated Calls, export, merge, and import. Every user needs an email. Screen recordings and digital interactions are not supported.

Vendors named for migrated history include NiCE Engage, CXone, Playback Portal, Uptivity, Verint, Genesys, Calabrio, Witness, Red Box, and Avaya. Anyone else is case by case.

Engage NMF converts to MP4 on playback. Teams are uploaded, not synchronized.
