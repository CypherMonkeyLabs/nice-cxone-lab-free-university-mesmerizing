# Use the floor

Two-voice architect briefing for the NICE CXone lab. Generated with espeak-ng and a quiet ffmpeg sine pad. Lesson bodies are not narrated.

- **Architect A:** Welcome to Contact Path. This is a free study lab for NICE CXone, not a vendor brochure.
- **Architect B:** The picture in front of you is the contact path. Point of contact, script, skill, agent, workforce, quality, and the API.
- **Architect A:** Use the study menu. Diagrams, Flows, and Charts are the big pictures. Cheat Codes and Downloads are the sheets you can keep offline.
- **Architect B:** Play is where you practise. Ecosystem is the map of neighbouring labs. Careers is a study path, not a job offer.
- **Architect A:** Open a track. The ring on the card is progress saved in this browser. Lesson text stays in English, next to its citation.
- **Architect B:** If motion is reduced, or WebGL is missing, the orbit becomes a flat drawing. The facts do not change.
